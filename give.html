Generosity

Every gift becomes a table, a teacher, and a testament.

We give because He first gave. Your generosity keeps the ministry running and the gospel going out — here and to the nations.

Two ways to give

In person, or by mail and transfer.

Every gift is recorded, receipted, and reviewed by our finance team and elders.

In person

Offering boxes are at the rear of the sanctuary during our Sunday service. Envelopes are available at the welcome desk.

Cash gifts can be receipted — simply include your name and address on the envelope.

By mail or transfer

Checks payable to Daily in Christ Fellowship, posted to the church office. Bank transfer details are available on request.

Stewardship

Where your giving goes.

We are committed to financial transparency and share our accounts with the congregation. Nothing is hidden, and no gift is taken for granted.

  • Ministry first. Most of what is given funds teaching, worship, children and youth, and pastoral care.
  • Mission always. A fixed portion goes to partners beyond our walls, here and abroad.
  • Open books. Accounts are reviewed by the elders and shared with the congregation.
  • No pressure. Guests are never asked to give. Come, receive, and belong first.

Mission Pledges

This fund is set aside for our upcoming mission trips. Pledge an amount and it goes straight to travel, food, and materials for the teams we send to partner churches and communities — here in the Philippines and beyond.

Questions

Giving, answered honestly.

Our finance team is happy to talk through anything here. Reach them at dailyinchristfellowship1999@gmail.com.

Are gifts tax-deductible?
Yes. Every gift is receipted at the end of the financial year. Please include your full name so we can match your gift to you and issue your receipt.
Can I give regularly?
Recurring gifts can be set up weekly, every two weeks, or monthly by bank transfer. Email the office and we will send you the details — you can change or stop them any time.
Can I designate my gift?
You may designate a gift to Missions or to the Building Fund. Undesignated gifts support the general ministry of the church, which gives us the flexibility to respond where the need is greatest.
Is my information secure?
Only the finance team and treasurer can see individual giving records, and they are kept in confidence. Bank details you send us are used only to process your gift, and we never share them with anyone.

Thank you

The generosity of this church is remarkable.

If you are new here, please do not feel any pressure to give. Come, receive, and belong first.
