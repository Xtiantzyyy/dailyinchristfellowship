Your first Sunday

Three things worth knowing

Arrive from 9:00

Doors open at 9:00, thirty minutes before the service. The welcome desk is straight ahead — someone will spot you.

Parking & access

Free parking behind the building, step-free entrances on both sides, and a hearing loop in the sanctuary.

Check-in for kids

Head to the Kids Wing five minutes before the service starts. Our team will walk you through check-in.
