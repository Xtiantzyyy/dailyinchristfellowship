What God Has Been Doing Among Us.

Highlights from 2026 — services, outreach, anniversaries, and gatherings from across our church family.

2026

Highlights from the Year

From candlelight in January to honoring our pastors in October — here is the year we shared together as one family in Christ.

January 4, 2026

Candlelight Service

We opened the year in candlelight — giving thanks for God’s faithfulness in 2025 and asking Him to lead us through the year ahead.

January 11, 2026

YA New Year Service

Young Adults gathered for their first service of the year, seeking God together and committing the months ahead to Him.

February 15, 2026

YA 23rd Anniversary — CLOSER

Young Adults celebrated 23 years with the theme “Closer” — a call to draw nearer to God and nearer to one another.

February 16, 2026

DCF Workers Go to Prayer Mountain

Our workers stepped away from the busyness of ministry for a day of prayer and seeking God together at Prayer Mountain.

March 31 – April 3, 2026

Reach Aurora for Jesus 2026

An outreach and missions trip to Brgy. Dicabasan, Dilasag, Aurora — sharing the Gospel and serving the community alongside local believers.

May 1, 2026

MIB & WOW One-Cell Gathering

Men in Bonding and Women of Worth came together as one cell for worship, the Word, and fellowship across both ministries.

May 10, 2026

Mother’s Day

We honored the mothers and mother-figures in our church family, giving thanks for their love, faith, and sacrifice.

May 13–16, 2026

VBS: Treasure Hunt 2026

Four days of Vacation Bible School under the theme “Treasure Hunt: Seeking the Riches of Christ” — “You will seek Me and find Me when you seek Me with all your heart.”

May 23, 2026

DCF Kapalangan 4th Year Anniversary

Our Kapalangan congregation marked four years of God’s faithfulness with a day of thanksgiving and celebration.

May 31, 2026

DCF 27th Anniversary

Daily in Christ Fellowship celebrated 27 years with the theme “Stronger Together as One Family in Christ.”

June 6, 2026

Y&A Summer Fellowship 2026

Young and Adult Fusion spent the day together at Bosay Resort in Antipolo, Rizal — fellowship, rest, and growing together in Christ.

June 12, 2026

Freedom to Pray

A church-wide call to prayer, gathering to intercede for our nation, our community, and one another.

June 21, 2026

Father’s Day

We gave thanks for the fathers and men who lead, provide, and point our families to Christ.

August 30, 2026

Friends and Family Sunday

A Sunday set aside to bring friends and family — worshiping together and sharing a meal afterward.

August 30–31, 2026

DCF Workers’ Team Building 2026

Two days at Rizal Recreation Center to rest, build team, and prepare our workers for the season ahead.

October 18, 2026

Pastor’s Appreciation Month

We honored our pastors and their families for their faithful service, thanking God for the way they shepherd our church.
