About Us

A Family That Takes Jesus at His Word.

We are not a building, a brand, or a program. We are people — forgiven, gathered, and sent — learning what it means to live every day in Christ.

Our Story

It Started with a Simple Prayer.

Daily in Christ Fellowship began with a handful of believers who wanted a church that took Jesus at His word — where the Bible was opened honestly, where prayer was not an afterthought, and where people were known by name.

We are not a large institution. We are a fellowship: families, students, workers, and grandparents who gather around the Word and around tables. Some of us have walked with Christ for decades; others are still asking their first questions. There is room for both.

Our prayer has not changed since those early days: Lord, teach us to walk with You daily — and to share our lives while we do.

Our Name

Three Words We Take Seriously.

Our name is a description of how we want to live — not a slogan, but something we are still growing into.

Daily

Not Occasional

Faith is not a Sunday event. It is a walk — morning by morning, in traffic, at work, at the table. We help one another keep going on the ordinary days.

In Christ

Our Identity

Before we are anything else, we are people who belong to Jesus. His life in us is the source of everything we do and the reason we keep gathering.

Fellowship

Shared Life

We share the Gospel with people, and then we share our lives with them. That second half is not optional — it is the whole point of 1 Thess. 2:8.

Mission & Vision

Why We Exist

Two statements, one direction — and one Lord at the center. Everything flows from Christ: the worship we offer, the Gospel we proclaim, the disciples we make, and the leaders we raise up in cell groups across the city.

Our Mission

To worship the living God as our highest service; commission every member to disciple the fruits of evangelism, commits to study the Word and to pray; and to establish cell groups in various locations.

Our Vision

We will win souls and make disciples by sharing the Gospel through evangelism, sharing the transformed lives through discipleship, and ultimately train Spirit-filled leaders.

What We Believe

Ancient Words, Held with Conviction.

We hold the historic beliefs of the Christian faith, simply stated. What follows is short enough to read over coffee and deep enough to spend a life on.

If you would like to talk through any of this — or disagree with it — our pastors would genuinely love that conversation.

Ask Us Anything
The Scriptures
We believe the Bible is the inspired, trustworthy Word of God — our final authority for faith and life. It is not a book about us, but a book about Him, and it is meant to be read, taught, and obeyed.
God: Father, Son, and Spirit
We believe in one God eternally existing in three persons — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — equal in power and glory, distinct in person and work.
Salvation by Grace
We believe every person has sinned and fallen short, and that salvation is a gift of God's grace received through repentance and faith in Jesus Christ — not earned by religious effort.
The Church
We believe the church is the body of Christ on earth: a people gathered for worship, equipped by the Word, and scattered for mission. Every member is a minister.
Baptism & Communion
We practice believer's baptism by immersion as a public declaration of faith, and we celebrate Communion regularly as a remembrance of the body and blood of Christ. Both are means of grace for the believing community.
The Holy Spirit
We believe the Spirit indwells, gifts, and empowers believers for holy living and bold witness, and that His fruit is the mark of a maturing Christian.
Mission & the Return of Christ
We believe Jesus will return to judge the living and the dead and to make all things new. Until then, we are sent — to our neighbors, our city, and the nations — with the good news of the kingdom.

Leadership

How We Are Led

A team, not a personality. Our church is led by elders, pastors, and ministry directors who are accountable to one another and to the congregation.

Elders

A plurality of elders gives spiritual oversight — teaching, shepherding, and guarding the doctrine of the church together rather than alone.

Pastors & Staff

Pastors and ministry directors carry the week-to-week work: preaching, pastoral care, children and youth, worship, and administration.

Volunteer Leaders

Small groups, kids, worship, and outreach are led by trained volunteers who serve alongside the pastors — every member a minister.

Want to meet the team? Come this Sunday — or say hello on our Facebook page.

Next Steps

Wherever You Are, There Is a Next Step.

You do not have to know everything, dress a certain way, or have your life sorted out before you begin. Start anywhere on this list.

Talk to Us
  1. Step 01

    Come to a Sunday Gathering

    Coffee from 9:00, a seat with your name on it, and no one will ask you to stand up.

  2. Step 02

    Say Hello

    Fill in a connection card or message our Facebook page — a real person will reply within the week.

  3. Step 03

    Join a Small Group

    Eight to twelve people meeting in homes across Quezon City to open the Word and share life.

  4. Step 04

    Serve and Share

    Find a team that fits your gifts, then begin sharing the Gospel and your life with the people around you.
