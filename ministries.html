Ministries

Something for Every Season — and a Family to Walk It with You.

Faith grows in community. Wherever you are in life — student, parent, grandparent, whether you were born here in Quezon City or arrived only recently — there is a group, a table, and a team waiting to welcome you.

Find Your Place

Eight Ways to Belong.

Every ministry here is led by volunteers who have been trained, screened, and — more importantly — changed by the gospel. It is common to find a whole family serving across two or three of them.

Praise and Worship

Singers, musicians, and worship leaders who lead the congregation into God's presence — from our Sunday gatherings to prayer nights. Rehearsal is discipleship too: we prepare our hearts before we prepare the songs.

Rehearsals · times on request

Multimedia

The team behind the screens: sound, livestream, lyrics, photography, and social media. If you are at home with a mixer, a camera, or a laptop — or simply willing to learn — there is a place for you here.

Serving every Sunday · training provided

Outreach & Missions

Feeding programs, visitation, and partner churches and workers here in Metro Manila and beyond. We go because Christ came to us first — and because the Gospel is good news for the whole neighborhood.

Serving days posted monthly

Kids for Christ (K4C)

Our Sunday school for nursery through Grade 6 — Bible stories, songs, crafts, and games, led by screened volunteers who know your child by name. Children are not only the church of tomorrow; they are part of the church today.

Sundays · 9:30 AM · Sunday school

Young and Adult Fusion

Our teens and young adults together in one ministry — worship, teaching, small groups, outings, and camp. Whether you are in senior high, in college, or in your first job, you do not have to figure out faith alone.

Sundays · 2:30 PM · Teens and young adults

Men in Bonding (MIB)

Fathers and men gathering for breakfast, Bible study, and practical service — because a home needs men who are being changed by Christ. Fathers, grandfathers, older brothers — you belong in this circle.

Meeting times on request · for fathers and men

Women of Worth (WOW)

Mothers and women of every season — a morning study with childcare, and an evening circle for those who work. Mothers, grandmothers, older sisters — you are seen, and you are worth the time.

Meeting times on request · for mothers and women

Small Groups

Our small groups are called cell groups — a handful of people who meet in a home during the week to pray, open Scripture, and carry one another's burdens. This is where the church stops being a crowd and becomes family.

Cell groups · in homes across the city · times vary

Serve

Nobody Sits on the Bench Here.

Every Sunday takes a small army of volunteers: greeters, baristas, teachers, musicians, ushers, sound engineers, and people who quietly pray through the building before the doors open.

You do not need to be an expert. You need to be willing — we will train you, and you will not serve alone.
